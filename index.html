    Race plan

    3:29:00, negative split

    Checkpoints

      If halfway is 1:46:30 or slower, sub-3:30 needs 4:50/km in the heat. Hold steady and decide again at 30K.

      Three rules

      1. Ease down across the first 10K. 5:10 → 5:00 is a slope, not 10 km at 5:10 (that's 51:40 and leaves 4:53/km for 32 km in the heat).
      2. No surge after 10K. km 11 is 4:57, not 4:50. Making up time at km 10–15 is how this plan fails.
      3. Hit the checkpoints. 10K in 50:30–51:00, half by 1:46:00, 30K around 2:29:20.

      Section by section

      km 0–10: the Loop → Lincoln Park
      5:10 → 5:00. GPS is useless in the Loop; trust the clock. Tuck in with the 3:35 group (Corral E, ~5:06/km) and edge ahead of it by km 8–10.
      km 10–21.1
      4:57 → 4:55. One smooth step down. Halfway 1:45:37, no slower than 1:46:00.
      km 21–30: West Loop → Pilsen
      4:55 → 4:54. The sun is on you: start cooling at aid stations. Don't chase the 3:30 pacers before 30K.
      km 30–32: decision point
      Hold the plan, 4:54 → 4:53. The 30K cue asks how you feel. Not strong? Hold 4:53 to the end.
      km 32–35, if strong
      4:48/km.
      km 35–40, if strong
      4:43/km. Chinatown → Michigan Ave, where you gain on the field, with the SE breeze at your back going north.
      After 40K
      Empty the tank. Projected finish about 3:27:15–3:27:30. From km 31 every split cue also says your projected finish.
      Back off
      Drop to 4:55 at cramps, chills or goosebumps, dizziness, or stomach trouble. The time banked by then still keeps you under 3:30.
      Mile 26 → finish
      Right onto Roosevelt Rd: a short climb over the rail yards, the only real hill. Left onto Columbus, ~300 m to go.

      Fuel

      Gel ~400 m before an aid station, chased with water. Take each 40 g gel in 2–3 squeezes over 30–60 s. Gatorade is optional.

      #kmClockWhat
      • Your stomach decides. If it complains, delay the next gel ~10 min. Never double up.
      • Skip the Maurten depots (mile 12.4, 18.1), and never the caffeinated CAF 100.
      • Capsules: swallow whole with a full cup of water, at stations where you aren't taking a gel. The third one is only for heat or heavy salt sweating.

      Heat

      • 60°F at the start, 68°F and sunny by 11. Expect 1–2% slower at equal effort.
      • 2–3 gulps of water at most stations. Drink to thirst, no more.
      • After ~25K, water over head and neck. Cooling zone near mile 21.2 (Cermak & Archer).
      • Heart rate will drift up. Hold effort, not exact pace, until 30K.
